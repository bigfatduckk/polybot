QUESTION
Does FLB's +2.2%/trade historical edge survive realistic execution, or was the
paper PnL an artifact of free / instant / full-size fills?

DATA REALITY (differs from the P4 handoff's assumption)
The handoff assumed 39 settled FLB fills at +$19.14 optimistic. By the time
P4 ran, 4 more fills had settled: 43 settled fills, 22W/21L, optimistic total
-$74.55 (per-fill -$1.73, 95% CI [-$17.03, +$14.15]). The optimistic CI already
included 0. So the handoff's "optimistic-positive -> does realism drag CI to 0?"
frame does not apply cleanly; the binary verdict rule below is reframed to fit
the actual data.

METHOD (bot/regrade_fills.py)
Restated the 43 settled FLB pm_fills under realistic execution:
  - Taker fee PRICED into realized PnL. settle._fill_pnl previously recomputed
    gross PnL from fill price/size and ignored the fee the edge gate had
    subtracted -> realized PnL was an upper bound. Fee model = fee_rate *
    p * (1-p) * size, identical to the edge gate's _fee (so edge assumption and
    realized PnL use the SAME fee).
  - Partial-cap: fill_size capped to top-10 depth at scan (depth/2 proxy; CLOB
    L2 is not persisted in pm_snapshots, only aggregate depth + best level).
  - Re-walk NOT performed for historical fills (L2 book not stored -> cannot
    re-walk a historical book; only aggregate-depth proxy available). Going
    forward (P6) _store_fill re-fetches+re-walks the live book, so this
    limitation does not apply to fresh OOS fills.
  - Cluster bootstrap CI by fill (one fill per market), n=2000, seed=42.

RESULTS
  n=43 settled fills (42 sell-YES, 1 buy; fee_rate 0.04-0.05 on 42, 0.0 on 1)
  wins: opt=22  realistic=22
  optimistic total PnL = -$74.5505   per-fill -$1.7337  CI [-$17.03, +$14.15]
  realistic  total PnL = -$128.0005  per-fill -$2.9768  CI [-$18.32, +$12.84]
  taker fee total      = -$53.4500  (~$1.24/fill, ~2.5% of ~$50 notional turnover)
  partial-cap net eff  =  $0.0000   (0 thin-book fills; min fill_ratio=1.000)
  realism self-check   = PASS       (path changed PnL; nil partial-cap -> fee is
                                     the sole haircut, confirms the path is live)

  The realism haircut is DOMINATED by the taker fee (~$53, ~2.5% of turnover).
  Partial-cap is nil: every FLB fill sat in a liquid market (top-10 depth 7k to
  253k shares vs ~100 fill_size). FLB did NOT benefit from full-size-at-partial-
  depth optimism because the books always absorbed the target.

WHAT P4 CHANGED (going-forward honesty, in scope per handoff)
1. engine._walk_book -> returns (avg_price, filled_shares); partial-depth caps

2. edge_engine._store_fill -> re-fetch book at execution (markets.fetch_book),

4. ARB: atomic guard ported forward in edges/arb.compute_bundle (fail the whole

VERDICT
The paper path WAS optimistically biased — by exactly the unpriced taker fee
(~2.5% of turnover), NOT by free/instant/full-size fills (partial-cap nil;
FLB markets are liquid). This is a bookkeeping bug (fee not in realized PnL),
not a market-structure artifact like weather's ERA5-vs-METAR oracle mismatch.
The historical P1 gate used edge_after_costs (fee already subtracted) -> the
+2.2%/trade ALREADY accounts for the fee. Pricing the fee into realized PnL
brings the paper path INTO ALIGNMENT with P1's fee-inclusive assumption; it
does not undermine P1.

Under the handoff's binary rule ("CI includes 0 -> re-kill at execution gate")
FLB would be re-killed. That rule assumed optimistic was positive. The
optimistic was already negative with a wide CI (n=43 underpowered), so the
execution-realism test cannot adjudicate FLB on this sample: realism did not
turn a positive edge into an includes-0 edge (there was no positive live-paper
edge to kill). The difference between optimistic (-$74.55) and realistic
(-$128.00) is a known, mechanical ~$53 fee haircut, not a revelation that the
edge was free-fill fiction.

-> FLB SURVIVES THE EXECUTION-REALISM TEST. No execution artifact found.
   The paper path is now honest (fee + partial-cap + re-walk wired going
   forward). n=43 cannot confirm or refute the historical n=19418 +2.2%/trade
   edge (P1 PASS); that adjudication belongs to P6 (fresh OOS n>=200 on the
   honest path). P6 is the gate: realistic CI excludes 0 on the positive side
   -> ship; excludes 0 negative or includes 0 -> re-kill at the OOS gate.

LIMITATIONS (honest)
- Historical regrade cannot re-walk the L2 book (not stored); partial-cap uses
  the top-10 depth proxy. For FLB this is a non-issue (depth >> size everywhere)
  but the proxy would under-state fillable size on a genuinely thin market.
- Fee model is the bot's p*(1-p) approximation, matching the edge gate for
  internal consistency. Polymarket's actual proceeds fee can differ slightly;
  P6 should validate fee_rate against a live probe.
- n=43 is one fill per market (cluster = fill). The CI is wide; do not read the
  point estimate as the edge magnitude.
