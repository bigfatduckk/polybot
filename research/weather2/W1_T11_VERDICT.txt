CHARACTERIZATION (corrected — the original "falsification: market embeds post-
processing" label was WRONG and is withdrawn):

The pre-reg's falsification clause anticipated a NO-SIGNAL failure: "EMOS has skill
BUT coef <= 0 OR p >= 0.05 -> market already embeds station post-processing -> class
closed permanently, no recalibration appeal." THAT MECHANISM DID NOT FIRE. The
statistical gate passed strongly (coef +0.18 @ p=0.0000) — the opposite of the
original weather kill (D-4: coef -0.069, p=0.47). The market does NOT fully embed
station post-processing; there is a genuine residual signal beyond price.

What failed is the ECONOMIC gate: the signal is real but too small to clear trading
costs. This is a different, narrower closure than the pre-reg anticipated — economic,
not epistemic.

THE COHERENCE CHECK (load-bearing): the two gates are the same finding viewed twice.
The regression says the market shrinks the model's disagreement to ~18% of face value
(coef 0.182 on logit(p_model) vs 1.01 on price). A 6¢ divergence at mid~0.5 is ~0.24
logit units; 0.18 x 0.24 x 0.25 ~= 1.1¢ of true edge per 6¢ face divergence. Costs
are ~2¢ half-spread + ~0.5-1¢ fee ~= 2.5-3¢. Predicted PnL ~= 1.1 - 2.75 ~= -1.7¢/trade,
vs measured -2.05¢ (CI [-3.45,-0.67]). The economic gate is not miscalibrated; it is
confirming the coefficient. (The pre-registered 6¢ raw-divergence rule was a strawman
execution — the regression's own coef says shrink 5x -> ~14-17¢ threshold — but that
does not rescue W1: fat-tail model error concentrates at large divergences, and their
frequency is tiny. See posterior-gap readout.)

VALIDITY NOTES (discount even the statistical pass slightly; both point same direction)
=====================================================================================

1. PIT maxdev 0.42 (median) is severe miscalibration of the predictive distribution.
   The logistic regression absorbs affine logit miscalibration so the coefficient
   survives, but the raw-divergence trade rule inherited the noise. A signal this
   discounted by the market is fragile.
2. Stale-quote contamination: the 00:00-local snapshot "mid" is probably last-trade,
   not a firm quote, so entry = mid +/- 2¢ is optimistic on thin books. Real execution
   would be WORSE, which strengthens the economic fail. Part of the +0.18 may be
   "predicting" quote updates nobody could reliably capture at firm prices.

=> The true monetizable signal is AT MOST what was measured. The +0.18 is real as
   information; the -2% is real as economics; they agree with each other.
